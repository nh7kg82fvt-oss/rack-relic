# Proposed Rack Relic seller payment rules

**Draft for owner and legal review. Not published, accepted by sellers, or an
active buyer/seller protection program.** Final wording must match provider
approval and working order, return and recovery flows before activation.

## Verification and listings

Sellers must complete the required Stripe identity and bank verification before
selling. Rack Relic can pause selling or payments if verification expires,
additional information is requested or suspicious activity needs review.

The initial pilot is limited to eligible adult sellers. A guardian-supported
path must be separately implemented and verified before enabling minor sellers.

Listings must accurately show the item, its condition, brand, defects and
authenticity. Counterfeits, replicas and other prohibited items are not allowed.
Sellers must possess and be able to supply the item they list.

## Initial pilot limits

The complete buyer order total, including shipping, tax and buyer fee, must not
exceed $100. Rack Relic permits one unsettled order across the marketplace at a
time during the initial pilot. Limits may increase only after a separate risk
and funding review. An order being paid or a seller receiving money does not
by itself remove all refund/dispute exposure.

## Shipping and records

Ship only after Rack Relic confirms the payment and permits fulfillment. Use
the order's approved tracked label and the delivery address saved with the
order. Do not use a different address supplied in a message. Address changes
require support review and the appropriate order/quote/payment workflow.

Keep accurate listing photos, item descriptions, shipping acceptance records,
tracking and relevant communications. Follow the shipping deadline disclosed
for the order. Report a shipping problem promptly through the order support
process. A test/sample label is never valid postage or proof of delivery.

## When seller money becomes available

Earnings remain pending until real carrier delivery has been verified and at
least two business days have passed, using Rack Relic's published calendar.
This is the earliest review time, not a guaranteed bank-arrival date.

An open buyer complaint, fraud review, refund, return, bank dispute,
verification problem or reconciliation issue keeps the relevant money on hold.
Rack Relic may also pause further selling while a seller's activity is reviewed.
For the initial pilot, an operator must review release; immediate/automatic
payouts are not offered. Stripe's availability and bank processing times apply.

## Refunds, disputes and recovery

Sellers must cooperate with requests for evidence and respond by the deadline
shown in the review. Refunds must use the original payment method through the
approved process. Do not send a separate payment to an account supplied in a
message, which can leave the original payment subject to another refund or
bank dispute.

Final terms must specify which party bears each refund, return-postage,
processing-fee and dispute cost. Where the seller is responsible under those
terms, Rack Relic may retain or adjust unpaid earnings or seek recovery only as
permitted by the provider agreements, the accepted terms and applicable law.
No bank/card debit permission is created by this draft.

Before a cost or recovery adjustment becomes final, the operational workflow
must record the basis, evidence, decision and communication, with a way for the
seller to request review. A clear fraud score or closed bank case alone does
not authorize release or prove that all accounting is resolved.

## Fees and customer rights

The owner-approved buyer platform fee for new orders is 5% of the item or combined bundle price, plus $0.50 once per order. A $25 item
therefore has a $1.75 platform fee; a bundle pays the fixed amount only once.
The buyer also pays actual shipping and applicable tax separately. Rack Relic
retains its platform fee for support, hosting, operations and reserves.

The seller bears actual payment processing and clearly disclosed, agreed
provider costs attributable to the seller's sales, including any selected extra
insurance. Examples can include attributable fraud screening, connected-account
and payout charges, subject to the final cost schedule. A monthly account cost
must be allocated only once to the applicable seller and billing period; it is
not a new charge on every item. Shared, unverified or unallocated costs cannot
silently reduce the seller's earnings.

Show the seller the applicable cost schedule, estimated earnings and any cost
reservation before the relevant commitment, record authenticated agreement to
its exact version, and reconcile actual provider evidence before releasing the
remaining earnings. Estimates do not establish a settled cost. Additional
insurance must be selected and agreed with its quoted cost before purchase.
Unknown costs or insufficient seller proceeds require a hold and review, not a
hidden deduction from the buyer fee, shipping funds or collected tax.

No seller subscription or paid fraud upgrade is introduced by this draft. The
buyer pays the approved $0.50 fixed component once per order, including bundles. Existing saved orders keep their original fee and
cost snapshot; a new fee policy cannot retrospectively reprice a payment.
Neither the retained platform fee nor passing through known costs guarantees
profit: refunds, unrecoverable disputes and other operating expenses can still
reduce Rack Relic's earnings. This proposed allocation is not yet active seller
acceptance or a production payment flow.

Rack Relic does not promise Depop's protection program, insurance, guaranteed
recovery or zero losses. Final policies must preserve applicable customer
rights and accurately describe the support and returns process Rack Relic can
actually operate.

## Required before using these rules

Owner/legal approval; complete cost allocation; approved shipping and return
deadlines; a supported release/recovery process; live provider eligibility;
authenticated seller acceptance of the exact final version; and an accessible
copy of the terms and privacy notice. Keep the draft out of public policy
routes and retain the release blocker until these are complete.
