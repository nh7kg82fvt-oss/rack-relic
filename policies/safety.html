Know what to look for, keep your information private, and get help when something feels wrong.

We’re still in preview. Checkout, payments, payouts and shipping-label purchases are closed. Do not send money to another member to buy a Rack Relic item. Refund coverage and transaction protection are not active.

For buyers

  • Read condition details, measurements and photos. Ask about missing information.
  • Use Report listing on an item for a suspected scam, counterfeit or misleading description.
  • Keep conversations on Rack Relic. Never share sign-in codes, financial details or ID with another member.
  • Use Block member in a conversation if you do not want further messages.

For sellers

  • Only offer items you own and may sell. Counterfeits, replicas and unauthorized copies are prohibited.
  • Show the actual item and its flaws. Keep original photos and accurate descriptions.
  • Keep addresses, identity documents and personal information out of listing photos.
  • Prepare drafts now. Do not accept payments or ship purchases during preview.

What happens after a report?

Listing reports go to private administrator review. A report is an allegation, not proof. It does not automatically remove an item, suspend a member or result in a refund. My reports shows your submitted reports and their status.

This helper can explain steps and prepare an email. It cannot decide whether an item is authentic, verify someone’s identity, issue refunds, hold payouts or resolve a dispute. Those decisions require review and, where applicable, the payment provider.

Before purchases open

We still need to finish seller verification and payouts, secure payment confirmation, inventory checks, tracked shipping, and order-linked refunds and disputes. The final reporting deadlines, return-shipping responsibilities and refund rules will be published before checkout opens. No buyer or seller guarantee is offered in this preview.

Talk to Rack Relic

Email support@rackrelic.com for help or privacy requests. Describe what happened and when; include a listing link if relevant. Do not email passwords, sign-in codes, card information or identity documents.
