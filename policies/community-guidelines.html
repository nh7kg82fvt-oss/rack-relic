Community guidelines

Rack Relic is open as a browsing and listing preview. Checkout is disabled. Do not send money, arrange an off-site payment or ship a purchase through this preview. Illustrated demo pieces are fictional and not for sale.

Describe the actual item

Only publish items you own and have the right to offer. Use photos you have permission to use and show the actual item, labels, measurements and flaws. Give an accurate brand, size and condition. Do not present stock images, illustrations or generated images as photos of an actual item.

Be honest about brands

Describe your item accurately, including its brand or source. Do not use misleading titles, inaccurate brand information or irrelevant hashtags. Adding an unrelated brand only to gain exposure is misleading, even if the description explains that it is not that brand.

Keep the racks safe

Counterfeits, replicas, unauthorized copies, stolen goods and items you cannot lawfully offer are prohibited. Do not publish another person’s private information, impersonate a person or business, post scams or spam, or harass or threaten members. Rack Relic does not authenticate every item.

Protect your account and privacy

Use Rack Relic messages to ask questions about real community listings. Never share passwords, sign-in codes, card or bank details, identity documents or home addresses in public listings. A message or screenshot is not proof of payment. There is no payment-release fee to pay during this preview.

Report a concern

Choose Report listing on a real listing to submit a report. Check My reports for its status. You can block a member from a conversation. Blocking prevents new messages between the accounts; existing conversations remain available.

Reports require review. A report is an allegation, and submitting one does not automatically remove an item or suspend a member. For account or privacy concerns, contact support@rackrelic.com. Include a listing link and a short description, without passwords, sign-in links or financial details.

Before purchases open

Seller verification, payment and shipping instructions, fees, dispatch deadlines, refund rules and dispute procedures will be available before checkout opens. No transaction protection, authenticity guarantee or guaranteed support response time is offered in this browsing preview.
