Preview help

RackRelic is preparing to launch in the United States. Checkout is not available yet. Marketplace transaction policies will be finalized before purchases open.

Illustrated demo items are not inventory for sale.

Can I buy something now?

No. Purchases, payment processing and payouts are not enabled.

What does a listing draft do?

A signed-in account draft saves privately to your account with up to six photos. Older local drafts stay on this device. Saving a draft does not publish it, buy an item or reserve inventory. Prepare a listing.

What will the buyer fee be?

When buying opens, the buyer platform fee will be 4% of the item price, with no fixed-dollar fee. Shipping and applicable tax are separate. Sellers bear actual payment processing costs; the processor and rate are not yet confirmed. Read about the preview.

Safety and reporting

Use our Safety & help center for suspicious listings, unsafe messages, account concerns and evidence checklists. It prepares next steps or a support email; it does not submit reports or make refund decisions.
