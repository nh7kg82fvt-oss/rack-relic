MAKE ROOM FOR SOMEONE ELSE’S NEXT FIND

Your first listing, thoughtfully prepared.

A clear photo, useful measurements and an honest description help someone understand your piece. Start with one item you own and have the right to sell.

We’re getting the racks ready. Checkout and payouts are not open yet. Prepare a draft now; wait for launch readiness before publishing real inventory or accepting purchases.

Three things to have ready

01 / THE PIECE

Check what you have

Confirm the item is available. Note the brand, label size, material, condition and any missing parts or repairs.

02 / THE DETAILS

Measure and describe

Give measurements with inches or centimeters. Describe fit without guessing and include every noticeable flaw.

03 / THE PHOTO

Show the real item

Use photos you have permission to use. Choose natural light and a plain background; avoid filters that hide color or wear.

A simple photo checklist

Full item

Keep the entire piece in frame, front and back.

Labels and details

Capture size, material and identifying features. A label alone does not prove authenticity.

Fit and scale

Show useful measurements and state the units.

Every flaw

Photograph stains, scuffs, loose seams and repairs.

Illustrated examples of what to capture, not photos of sale inventory. The current local draft holds one photo; keep your other photos ready separately. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB.

Keep faces, addresses, receipts with personal information and other private details out of the image.

Choose condition honestly

New with tags
Unworn, with the original tags attached.
Like new
No noticeable wear or flaws.
Good
Visible light wear. Describe any marks or repairs.
Fair
Clear wear or flaws. Explain the condition and photograph the damage.

If you’re between two options, explain the wear in your description so the next person can decide.

Make the description useful

For clothing, include the label size and relevant measurements such as chest width, length or inseam. For shoes, state the size system and show the soles. For bags, include dimensions and what comes with the item.

Example only — not an actual listing Vintage denim jacket, label size M. Measurements laid flat: chest 21 in; length 25 in. Light fading at the cuffs and a small mark on the left sleeve, shown in the photo. No missing buttons. Jacket only; no accessories included.

Counterfeits, replicas and unauthorized copies are prohibited, even when disclosed. Rack Relic does not authenticate every item.

Save a draft, then come back to it

  1. Enter the title, brand, category, price, size, condition and description.
  2. Add an optional photo. You can replace it or remove it before saving.
  3. Select Save draft on this device. Find it under Sell, then choose Edit draft to make changes.

A local draft stays in this browser. It is not a published listing and does not reserve an item. People using the same browser profile may see it; clearing browser data may remove it. An account copy is a separate step, and the local photo is not copied automatically.
