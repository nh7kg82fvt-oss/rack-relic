Fees at a glance

The buyer pays the 5% + $0.50 platform fee. The percentage applies to the order’s item subtotal; $0.50 is added once per order. Shipping and tax are separate.

No seller platform fee. The buyer platform fee is added at checkout, not deducted from your item price.

Example: list an item for $25 and the buyer pays $26.75, plus shipping and tax.

Seller processing & handling: separate from the buyer fee. Actual costs and your estimated payout must be shown for your review and acceptance before a sale. Optional shipping insurance costs extra only if you choose it. Final cost details will be available before buying opens.

A little extra help

Photos and condition

Signed-in account drafts support up to six photos: one cover and five more views. Device-only drafts keep one cover photo. Use JPG, PNG or WebP files, up to 2 MB each.

New with tags: unworn, original tags attached. Like new: no noticeable wear. Good: light wear. Fair: clear wear or flaws. Describe and photograph any damage.

For clothes, add flat measurements; for shoes, include the size system and show the soles; for bags, give dimensions and what is included.

Drafts and listing rules

Find saved drafts under Sell, then choose Edit draft. A device-only draft stays in this browser, may be visible to others using it and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Saving it to your account is a separate step; its local photo is not copied automatically.

Use photos you have permission to use. Titles, brand fields, descriptions and tags must match the item. Unrelated brands added for exposure are prohibited, even with a disclaimer. Counterfeits, replicas and unauthorized copies are prohibited, even when disclosed. Rack Relic does not authenticate every item.

See the community guidelines for the full rules.

Shipping and staying safe

When buying opens, buyers will pay shipping at checkout. Sellers will pack and send items using the label provided for the order. Carrier options, prices, shipping deadlines and return instructions will be available before checkout opens.

Live labels are not available yet. Test labels are not valid postage.

After launch, ship only when Rack Relic confirms a paid order. Messages and payment screenshots do not confirm payment. Never share sign-in codes or pay to “release” a sale.
