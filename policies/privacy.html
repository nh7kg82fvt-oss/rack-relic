Privacy notice

Updated October 31, 2026. This notice describes the current Rack Relic browsing and listing preview, operated by Rack Relic LLC.

Purchases, payment collection, payouts and shipping-label purchases are disabled in the public preview. Do not submit card details, bank details, identity documents or a shipping address through listings, messages or support email.

Information used by the preview

  • Accounts: the email address used to sign in and the public seller name and optional bio you save.
  • Listings: titles, brand, category, price, size, condition, descriptions, photos and delivery preferences you choose to save. Publishing makes the listing and seller profile visible to visitors; private account drafts are separate.
  • Messages and safety: conversations with other members, saved listings, listing reports and member blocks. Messages are visible to conversation participants. Reports are submitted for private administrator review.
  • Device storage: sign-in sessions, device-only drafts and photos, saved demo pieces, saved searches and privacy choices. People using the same browser profile may see device-only data. Clearing browser storage can remove it.
  • Support: the email and information you send when requesting help. A help-widget email is only a prepared draft until you send it in your email application.

Why this information is used

To sign you in, show and manage listings and profiles, deliver messages, save your choices, investigate reports, provide support and protect the service. Do not put private contact details in public profiles, descriptions or photos.

Services used to run Rack Relic

Vercel hosts the website. Supabase provides account sign-in, database and photo storage. These services process the information needed to deliver those features and may process technical request information such as an IP address and browser details. Email support uses the Rack Relic mailbox service. Other members receive the listing, profile or message information you choose to share with them.

Optional Google Analytics is controlled by your browser choice. Advertising tracking is disabled. See Privacy & tracking choices for measurement details and controls. These policy pages do not load optional analytics.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile and drafts, unpublish or archive your listings, and manage saved listings and blocked members in your account. Archiving hides a listing; it is not permanent deletion. Device-only drafts are managed in the browser where they were created.

For access, correction or deletion requests, email support@rackrelic.com from the address associated with your account and describe the request. We may need to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. The preview does not have a self-service account-deletion button.

Retention and limits

Account records remain stored unless changed or removed through the service or a reviewed support request. Device-only data remains until you remove it or clear browser storage. We have not introduced a general automatic deletion schedule for preview account records. Support can explain which records can be removed and any records that need to be retained for security or resolving a reported issue.

No service can promise complete security. Keep your account secure, avoid sharing sign-in links and report suspicious activity through Safety & help. Changes that introduce purchases or new data uses will require an updated notice before those features open.
