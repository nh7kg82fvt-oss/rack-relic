Privacy & tracking choices

Rack Relic is operated by Clear Choice Payments LLC. This page explains optional website measurement during our marketplace preview. Checkout is not open.

What is essential

Sign-in sessions, saved drafts and your privacy choices use browser storage so the site can work. Rejecting optional analytics does not disable those features.

Optional analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand public page visits, browsing categories, use of search, visits to available listings, and interest in selling. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. Google also receives technical information such as browser, device and network information when your browser connects to them.

We exclude raw search text, sign-in links, account identifiers, email addresses, private drafts, messages and photo files from our analytics event data. Page addresses are filtered before measurement. Search use is counted without sending what you typed.

Advertising is not active

Advertising pixels, advertising cookies and retargeting are disabled during this setup. We will ask for a new choice before activating them. Google advertising storage, advertising user data and personalization remain denied.

Change your choice

Use Privacy & cookie choices at the bottom of any page to allow or reject optional analytics. We remember your choice on this browser for up to 180 days. Clearing browser storage or using another device may require choosing again. Withdrawing consent stops future optional measurement; it does not erase reports already received by a provider.

Global Privacy Control keeps advertising off. We do not promise that every visit can be measured: browser settings, blockers and your choice can prevent collection.
