Add Rack Relic to your phone

Use your browser's menu to add Rack Relic to your Home Screen.

iPhone — Safari

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll the menu or open the browser menu first.
  3. If Open as Web App appears, turn it on.
  4. Tap Add, then open Rack Relic from its Home Screen icon.

Android — Chrome

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu (three dots).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen or Install app, if available.
  4. Follow your browser's confirmation steps.

Menu options vary by device and browser. This guides you through adding the existing website; it does not install automatically. No App Store download is required. Internet access is required.

    First-listing guide: photos, sizing and condition

    Help build the first racks

    Help build Rack Relic's first racks. Prepare an item you have the right to sell, photos you may use, and an honest description of its condition and flaws. Counterfeit, replica and unauthorized copies are prohibited, even when disclosed. Rack Relic does not authenticate every item. Payment and payout setup remain pending.

    • Prepare accurate shop/profile details.
    • Confirm ownership and actual item availability.
    • Prepare permitted photos and disclose flaws.
    • Record category, size, condition, price and supported delivery options.
    • Wait for image-privacy acceptance before publishing real inventory.
    • Wait for verified checkout/payment onboarding before accepting purchases.

    Your next find starts here.

    Save a draft on this device, then copy its text details to your account. Add a photo to your account draft, then publish when ready.

    1. Add your photo

    Use your own clear photo in natural light. Show the full item, labels and any flaws. Photos saved here stay in this browser; they are not uploaded to your account.

    2. Describe your piece

    Choose the closest description. Mention every visible flaw, even if it is small.

    How should I describe condition?
    • New with tags: unworn, with the original tags attached.
    • Like new: no noticeable wear or flaws.
    • Good: visible light wear; describe marks or repairs.
    • Fair: clear wear or flaws; explain what still works and photograph the damage.

    Include the label size and useful measurements, with inches or centimeters. Do not include your home address or private contact details.
